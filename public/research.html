Jovian Polar Vortex Generation in a Shallow-Water Model with Quasi-Stochastic Convection

December 2025 – present

Advised by Hao Fu

I developed a polar γ-plane shallow-water solver with vorticity-dependent, quasi-stochastic convective parameterization to test how convective feedback affects the formation of polar vortex crystals. The simulations reproduced shielded cyclones and persistent vortex crystals. The research confirmed that increasing mesoscale feedback promotes vortex growth, shield formation, and spectral organization, while the characteristic vortex scale depends on both the deformation radius and the convective parameterization. A Biot–Savart diagnostic reveals competing contributions to structural drift, with predominantly counterclockwise advection induced by the other vortices and clockwise advection induced by the background vorticity outside the identified vortices.

arXiv:2611.02372

Advised by Hao FuUndergraduate Research Training Program

Internal variability in East Asian summer monsoon projections

June – August 2025

Advised by Sandro M. Ferreira VeigaSino-Finnish Research Training Program

I used empirical orthogonal function analysis of North Pacific sea surface temperature anomalies to identify phases of the Pacific Decadal Oscillation (PDO), then compared rainfall composites from CMIP6 MIROC6 simulations. Projected rainfall varied across three emissions scenarios, and opposite PDO phases produced broadly opposing rainfall anomalies over eastern China.
