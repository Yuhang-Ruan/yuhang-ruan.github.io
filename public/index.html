About

Nice to meet you! I’m Yuhang Ruan (阮渝航), a fourth-year undergraduate in the dual-degree programme in atmospheric sciences at Nanjing University and the University of Helsinki. I work under the supervision of Prof. Hao Fu.

I’m drawn to the idea that the same physical principles can explain very different natural phenomena. My current work is in geophysical and astrophysical fluid dynamics (GAFD).

I’m interested in how fluids move on Earth and across other worlds. On Earth, I focus on convection under different conditions and on how flows at different scales interact in the atmosphere and oceans. In planetary science, I’m particularly interested in Jupiter’s polar vortices and jets, the general circulation of gas giants, and planetary climates. I also find astrophysical disks and stellar atmospheres fascinating, and I’ve studied magnetohydrodynamics to better understand them. The mathematics behind these phenomena is just as compelling to me. I’ve taken a range of mathematics courses and hope to apply what I’ve learned in future research.

I also work on laboratory fluid experiments and numerical modelling. Since summer 2025, I’ve been a member of the Student Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Lab at Nanjing University.
