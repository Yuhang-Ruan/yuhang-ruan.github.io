About

I am Yuhang Ruan, a fourth-year undergraduate in the dual-degree program in atmospheric sciences at Nanjing University and the University of Helsinki, advised by Prof. Hao Fu. My research is in geophysical and planetary fluid dynamics, with a focus on how convection and vortices interact across scales.

My recent work focuses on the formation and dynamics of Jupiter’s polar vortices. I developed a polar γ-plane shallow-water solver to investigate how mesoscale convective feedback affects vortex growth, shielding, and collective drift. More broadly, I am interested in convective organization, large-scale circulation, and planetary climates.

I combine idealized numerical modeling, theoretical analysis, and rotating-fluid experiments. My coursework in partial differential equations, analysis, and theoretical physics supports my modeling work. I conduct rotating-tank experiments in Nanjing University’s Student Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Lab.
