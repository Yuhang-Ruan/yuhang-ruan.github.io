Jovian Polar Vortex Generation in a Shallow-Water Model with Quasi-Stochastic Convection

Yuhang Ruan, Hao Fu, Cheng Li & Da Yang (2026)

Submitted to The Planetary Science Journal.

arXiv:2611.02372Simulation video

Research summary

Polar γ-plane shallow-water simulations show that vorticity-dependent convective feedback can help form shielded cyclones and persistent vortex crystals. The diagnostics identify opposing effects from the other vortices and the background vorticity on collective drift.
